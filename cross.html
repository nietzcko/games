Barriers: hop them at speed or run them. Stairs: always run. Sand: you roll in on momentum, but pedalling in sand drains energy fast. Once you're slower than running pace, jump off and run. Mud: stay steady or your wheel spins. Go into a tight corner too hot and you drift: the faster you are, the more the slide scrubs off.

Keyboard: left and right arrows move the slider, 1 to 4 jump to a zone, space to hop.
